What an estate planning attorney can help with

An estate plan puts your instructions into a legal framework and identifies who you want to carry out key responsibilities. Depending on your situation, that may involve a will, a trust or questions about probate. If you are looking for an attorney for estate planning Leawood, start by describing what you want the plan to accomplish, not by assuming you need a particular document.

Some people want to clarify who should receive property. Others have questions about trust arrangements or what happens during probate. An attorney can help sort out which concerns call for legal planning and explain the choices in terms you can use.

Key facts, steps and useful terms

  • What the service covers: Advice may concern wills, trusts, probate and trust law. The documents or legal work needed depend on your goals and circumstances.
  • Typical timing: There is no set schedule that applies to every client. Time can vary with the complexity of the plan, the number of decisions involved and how promptly information is provided.
  • Cost in general: The fee depends on the scope and complexity of the legal work. Ask what the proposed work includes, how fees are calculated and whether additional work could change the total. No particular price should be assumed without discussing your matter.
  • Comparing attorneys: Ask about experience with the specific issues you need to address, how the attorney will explain options, who will handle communication and what work is included. Compare the clarity of the answers and the proposed scope, rather than selecting a provider based only on a document name.

A will sets out instructions that generally apply after death. A trust is a legal arrangement for managing property. Probate refers to a court-supervised process that may be part of settling an estate. Ask the attorney how each term relates to your own questions.
