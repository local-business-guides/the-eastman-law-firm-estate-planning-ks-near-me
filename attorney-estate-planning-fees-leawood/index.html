What the work and fee factors may include

Estate planning can involve advice about wills, trusts, and the broader structure of a plan. Depending on your needs, you may also have questions related to probate or trust law. The Eastman Law Firm can provide counsel in these areas, while the specific work depends on your circumstances.

Use these points to organize a fee discussion:
- Scope: Ask which planning tasks and documents are included in the proposed work.
- Complexity: The number of issues to address and the details of your circumstances may affect how much legal work is needed.
- Timing: The schedule can vary based on the plan's scope, decisions to be made, and when relevant information is ready. Ask what timing is reasonable for your matter.
- Fee arrangement: Ask how the fee is determined and when you will be told if the expected scope changes. No particular amount can be estimated without knowing the work involved.
